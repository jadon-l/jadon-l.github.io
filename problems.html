(JOM (Junior Olympiad of Malaysia) 2025/2) Let \(\Gamma\) be a fixed circle, and \(B\), \(C\) be two fixed points on \(\Gamma\). Let \(A\) be any point on \(\Gamma\) other than \(B\) and \(C\). Points \(B'\) and \(C'\) are chosen on \(\Gamma\) such that \(BB'\parallel AC\) and \(CC' \parallel AB\). Let \(E\) be on \(CA\) and \(F\) be on \(AB\) such that \(BE\perp CA\) and \(CF\perp AB\). Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be the intersections of the line \(EF\) with \(AC'\) and \(AB'\) respectively.

Prove that regardless of the choice of $A$ on $\Gamma$, the line passing through the two intersections of the circles \((AEX)\) and \((AFY)\) passes through a fixed point.
